TO DO APP - HOW TO INSTALL AND UPDATE
=====================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png  -> the app (upload once)
  todo.xlsx  -> your to-do list (edit and re-upload whenever it changes)

IMPORTANT - YOUR LIST IS PUBLIC
  Free GitHub Pages only works with a PUBLIC repository, so anyone who knows (or guesses)
  the web address can open todo.xlsx. Don't put anything private in it: no passwords,
  account numbers, addresses, phone numbers, or confidential work details.
  Your ticks are NOT uploaded - they stay on your phone.

ONE-TIME SET-UP (about 5 minutes, on your computer)
  1. Sign in at github.com.
  2. Click + (top right) > New repository. Name it: to-do
     Choose PUBLIC. Click Create repository.
  3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in ALL 7 files from this folder
     (including todo.xlsx). Click Commit changes.
  4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment", Source: Deploy from a branch,
     Branch: main, folder: / (root). Click Save.
  5. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your app is at:  https://<your-github-username>.github.io/to-do/

INSTALL ON YOUR IPHONE
  1. Open the address above in Safari.
  2. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
  3. Always open it from the green To Do icon.

UPDATING YOUR LIST (any time)
  1. Open todo.xlsx in Excel and edit the Tasks tab (see the "How to use" tab).
  2. Save it - keep the name exactly todo.xlsx and keep it as an Excel workbook (.xlsx).
  3. On github.com, open your to-do repository > Add file > Upload files.
     Drag in todo.xlsx (it replaces the old one). Click Commit changes.
  4. Wait 1-2 minutes, then open the app (or tap the blue refresh button).
     A message shows how many tasks were added or removed.
  You can also do step 3 from your iPhone in Safari: github.com > repository > Add file > Upload files.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - The app checks for a new todo.xlsx each time you open it. With no signal it shows
    the last list it downloaded.
  - Ticks are linked to the List + Section + Task name. Renaming a task (or moving it to
    another list/section) starts it fresh. Changing Qty, Due, Priority, Steps, Notes or
    Link keeps the ticks.
  - Steps you add inside the app are saved on your phone only (not in the Excel file).
  - Back up your ticks now and then: ••• > Backup & restore. Deleting the Home Screen
    icon deletes its ticks.
  - Needs iOS 16.4 or later.
  - The Shot List app can live in the same GitHub account (its own repository); the two
    don't affect each other.
